WHO CARDIOVASCULAR RISK MANAGEMENT GUIDELINES
Section 1: Blood Pressure Thresholds
1. Hypertension is defined as systolic blood pressure (SBP) greater than or equal to 140 mmHg and/or diastolic blood pressure (DBP) greater than or equal to 90 mmHg.
2. For patients with SBP between 130-139 mmHg or DBP between 80-89 mmHg, lifestyle modifications (weight loss, sodium reduction, physical exercise) are recommended.
3. Pharmacological treatment is indicated for adults with SBP >= 140 or DBP >= 90 when lifestyle interventions fail.

Section 2: Cardiovascular Risk Evaluation
1. Total cholesterol and HDL levels should be assessed.
2. Patients with diabetes or chronic kidney disease are classified as high cardiovascular risk, regardless of other factors.
3. Smoking status should be recorded and cessation advice offered to all smokers.
